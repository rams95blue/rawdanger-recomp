# rawdanger-recomp: the textures the US release changed, for the Enhanced Edition's Japanese look options (rd_hybrid.py
# --jp-textures faces,signs). Name in ROOT.FAT, then the group:
#   faces  the characters as the Japanese release draws them (black hair, darker eyes and skin, the original clothes colours)
#   signs  writing in the world in Japanese: shop signs, packets, keys, papers, the game-over screen
# Made by comparing both discs (same name, other bytes); 151 textures. Other files that differ (maps, models, overlays) are
# not swapped: they go with each release's own program code.
#   logos  the Japanese title logo (絶体絶命都市2) where the US one says RAW DANGER: at the start and behind the title menu
#          (BOOT.DAT picture 34, TITLE.DAT picture 8; the title menu's words stay English)
#   movies the Japanese opening film (Japanese logo, captions and voices, the original hair) and the other film that differs
# A fourth column "tims=N,..." takes only those pictures from the Japanese file and keeps the rest of the US one.
# A third column: a texture only the US disc has, a copy of a redrawn one; its Japanese version is that one's. The US program
# draws Joshua (the player) from TEX/22103 and TEX/22041, copies of TEX/10033 and TEX/10092 (found by matching the textures
# drawn in the banquet hall to the disc).
